## Content focus (automation)

The workflow now fetches:

- **Politics** — NewsAPI `everything` (US elections, Congress, White House, etc.)
- **Celebrities** — NewsAPI `entertainment` top headlines (US)

Rough mix: up to **6 politics + 6 celebrity** stories per day (10 posts total).

---

## Visual brand (images)

Every AI image uses the same **yellow · red · green** palette:

- Warm **yellow** highlights (light, warmth)
- **Red** accents (energy, urgency)
- **Deep green** tones (background, clothing, decor)

Style: **photorealistic, candid press photo** — natural, not cartoon, not graphic design.

**Emojis:** **1–2 per caption** (Facebook text), not burned into the image (looks more natural and avoids Meta issues).
